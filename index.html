How to practise

  1. Select a target note.
  2. Tap Start microphone.
  3. Sing “aa” gently and hold the note.
  4. Try to bring the cents reading close to 0.
  5. Repeat 5 times, resting between attempts.

Green means close (within 10 cents). This is an estimate from your phone microphone; background noise and other instruments can affect it. It measures pitch, not exact vocal tone quality.
